Privacy Policy for Break Though

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Break Though is a casual brick-breaker game. This policy explains what data the app collects. In short: none.

Data Collection

Break Though does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertisements, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs in version 1.0.

On-Device Data Only

Your game progress — current level, stars earned, and scores — is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children under 13 either.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in the future, for example when new features are added. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
