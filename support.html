Break Though Support

Break Though is a fast, casual brick-breaker with 25 handcrafted levels. Drag the paddle with one finger, keep the ball in play, and clear every brick.

How to Play

  • Drag the paddle left and right to bounce the ball.
  • Clear all bricks to finish a level. You have 3 lives per level.
  • Some bricks hide rewards: multiball, a longer paddle, a slower ball, piercing shots, and extra lives.
  • Your progress (levels and stars) saves automatically on your device, and the game works fully offline.
